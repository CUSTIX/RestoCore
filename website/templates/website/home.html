(01) Our story

A neighbourhood café that takes its time.

We started with one espresso machine and a simple idea: good coffee is worth waiting for. Today we roast our own beans, laminate croissants over three days, and cook brunch from scratch, all day long.

“Bring a book, bring the laptop, bring the dog. Refills of brewed coffee are on us before ten.”
